## In short

HorseShowPrep keeps your data **on your device**. There is no account, no server run by us,
no tracking, no advertising and no analytics libraries.

Two features reach beyond your device, and only these two — and only once you use them:

1. **Travel time and map:** When you request a route or search for an address, the start and
   destination addresses are sent to **Apple Maps**. Without this, no travel time can be
   calculated. The app does **not** request your location.
2. **Calendar:** If you switch on the calendar connection, the app writes events into its
   own calendar named "HorseShowPrep". It has **write access only** and **never reads your
   existing events**.

## What the app stores

Horses (name, optionally a photo and notes), shows (title, date, start time, venue and
address), your preparation templates and your home stable. All of it lives in the app's own
database on your device and is included in your encrypted iPhone backup, if you make one.

**No iCloud sync:** The current version does not synchronise anything between devices. If
you delete the app, the data is gone.

## Permissions

| Permission | What for | When you are asked |
|---|---|---|
| **Notifications** | Reminders for preparation tasks | The first time a reminder is scheduled |
| **Calendar (write only)** | Adding your shows to your calendar | Only when you switch the calendar connection on |

The app requests **no** location access, **no** photo library access (you pick horse photos
through the system picker, which does not give the app access to your library), **no**
microphone and **no** read access to your calendar.

If you decline a permission or withdraw it later, the app remains fully usable — only that
particular feature stops working.

## Sharing with third parties

None. There are no advertising networks, no analytics services and no sale of data. The only
transmission is the address lookup sent to Apple Maps described above; Apple's privacy policy
applies to that.

## Deleting your data

Delete individual entries inside the app. To remove every calendar event the app created,
use "Remove all calendar entries" in Settings — this deletes the "HorseShowPrep" calendar
with all its entries and leaves your other calendars untouched. Deleting the app removes all
local data with it.

## TestFlight (beta phase only)

TestFlight feedback (screenshots, comments, device data) is processed by Apple under the
TestFlight terms and made available to the developer for fixing problems. It is deleted from
development systems after 30 days at the latest.
